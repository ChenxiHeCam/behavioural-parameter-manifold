# Cover letter — Nature Communications (transfer or direct submission)

Chenxi He
Cavendish Laboratory, University of Cambridge

Dear Editors,

Please consider the enclosed manuscript, **"How much of a nervous-system model does behaviour
identify?"**, for publication as an Article in *Nature Communications*. I am the sole author, and
the work is not under consideration elsewhere.

Whole-organism simulators — connectome-constrained nematode models, musculoskeletal fly models —
are calibrated against behaviour and then read as the animal's biophysics. That reading assumes
behaviour determines the parameters that produce it, and at connectome scale that assumption had
never been measured. We measure it. Perturbing each of the 3076 connectome weights of a published
*C. elegans* model and reading the motor command through 9600 observables, eight directions carry
90% of the curvature and 47 carry 99%; only four weights are without measurable effect. A
*Drosophila* visual model concentrates its curvature the same way. What is determined and what is
free has biological identity — chemical synapses carry 87% of the curvature, and the free
conductances, the calcium currents EGL-19 and UNC-2, are those biology itself lets vary between
animals. Chemotaxis and locomotion render different axes stiff, and each reaches directions the
other cannot, so the identified set grows with the diversity of actions elicited rather than with
recording duration. At the cell scale, where ground truth exists, identified models are recovered
exactly while the canonical MAPK cascade matches behaviour with its parameters 40% wrong: a
perfect behavioural fit does not imply recovered parameters.

Every reported dimension carries three admission conditions — observables outnumbering parameters,
response proportional to the step, signal above the simulator's own noise — and the conditions
retired one of our own headline numbers. The release deposits one result file per analysis, a
container that re-derives the audited quantities, and a specification table for every assay.

Four independent biological datasets that use none of our simulators — the enumerated pyloric
population, Allen Cell Types electrophysiology, ion-channel-gene conservation, and isogenic
behavioural variation — order what is constrained and what is free consistently with the models.

Thank you for your consideration.

Chenxi He
